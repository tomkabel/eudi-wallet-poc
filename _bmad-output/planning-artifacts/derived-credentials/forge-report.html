Verify the PID, then issue a derived attestation

bmad-forge-idea, run autonomously on 2026-11-06. Every decision was made with tiltrank. Full brief: forge-brief.md

Outcome

Mostly no to the post's thesis; a narrow yes for this repository. Next step: bmad-correct-course, adding one gated story to Epic 10 after Stories 3.1 and 10.1. The close runner-up was parking it.

Locked

  • A derived attestation is worth issuing only for a fact the PID cannot carry: standing, role, mandate. Re-asserting identity is a non-goal.
  • First slice: the issuer authenticates the holder by an OpenID4VP presentation of the PID nested inside OpenID4VCI (spec §11.5), then issues a mock mandate attestation without PID attributes. The wallet refuses an issuer that asks for more than its registered intended use.

Rejected, and why

  • A separate product: no payer (the wallet is free to holders, issuers cannot meter verifications without breaking unlinkability, Art. 45h bars reusing the data) and a two-sided cold start (Mobile Connect).
  • Bank attestation that customer due diligence was done: AMLR Art. 48–49 keep responsibility with the bank that relies on it, and banks can identify with the PID directly (Art. 22(6)(b)).
  • Telco account attestation as the demo payload: it is not in the spec catalogue, and KYC Match already sells the same fact.

Weak points that survived

  • The verdict was contested: parking costs nothing while Epics 3, 4 and 10 are unstarted.
  • Unknown whether the wallet can handle a presentation request in the middle of an issuance.
  • In Estonia, mandates belong to the state (Pääsuke), so the slice does not test the "businesses issue" thesis.
