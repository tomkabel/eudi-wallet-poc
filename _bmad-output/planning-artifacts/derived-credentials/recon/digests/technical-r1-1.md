## Findings

### Q1: OpenID4VCI 1.0 final and the IAE

- OpenID4VCI 1.0 is a Final specification dated 16 September 2025; the approved text is also available at `openid-4-verifiable-credential-issuance-1_0-final.html` | https://openid.net/specs/openid-4-verifiable-credential-issuance-1_0.html | OpenID Foundation | 2025-09-16 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | spec
- OpenID4VCI 1.0 has no Interactive Authorization Endpoint. A full-text grep of the final spec finds no "interactive" or "OpenID4VP" mechanism. Presenting a credential during issuance appears only as a non-normative use case: Appendix H.5, "Wallet-Initiated Issuance during Presentation (Requires Presentation of Additional Credentials During Issuance)", in which the End-User is redirected to the Wallet to present and then back to the issuer's web experience. The normative hook is the ordinary Authorization Endpoint (§5, §5.1 Authorization Request, `issuer_state`), and how the issuer authenticates the user there is out of scope | https://openid.net/specs/openid-4-verifiable-credential-issuance-1_0.html#appendix-H.5 | OpenID Foundation | 2025-09-16 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | spec
- The IAE was merged into the draft in mid-2025 and then removed before 1.0 final "to give it the required time" because of open security questions. It moves to 1.1 (cre8 and jogu comments of 2025-09-24; issue #473 closed 2025-10-16) | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/issues/473 | OpenID Foundation DCP WG (GitHub) | 2025-03-25 to 2025-10-16 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | spec
- The IAE now lives in "OpenID for Verifiable Credential Issuance 1.1 - Editor's draft" (repo path `1.1/openid-4-verifiable-credential-issuance-1_1.md`, last commit 2026-10-15). It contains §"Interactive Authorization" (`#interactive-authorization`), "Require Presentation" (`#ia-require-presentation`), "Preventing Forwarding of Interactive Authorization Presentation Requests", per-format "Interactive Authorization Binding" sections, and the `interactive_authorization_endpoint` AS metadata. The text is not final. Open issues as of today include #742 (cross-session holder-binding confusion, Tamarin-verified attack on the editor's draft), #811 (draft -01 spells the `ia`/`iae` identifiers two ways) and #819 (`error` vs `error_code`) | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/tree/main/1.1 | OpenID Foundation | 2026-10-15 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | spec
- The 1.1 IAE was rebased onto OAuth First-Party Apps (PR #736, merged 2026-06-24). `auth_session` was made optional (PR #706, merged 2026-08-27) and `require_interactive_authorization_requests` was added to AS metadata (PR #615) | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/pulls?q=interactive | OpenID Foundation | 2025-11 to 2026-08 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | spec

### Q3: Reference issuer and reference wallet

- The reference issuer eudi-srv-web-issuing-eudiw-py lists "Dynamic Credential Request ✅" in its README feature table. Its changelog says "Support dynamic-credential-request - OID4VCI draft 13". ### Q4: Same-device feasibility and pitfalls

- OIDF issue #730, "Best Practices for Credential Presentation during Issuance with VCI 1.0" (open, 2026-04-15), lists three variants: (1) separated flows, VP first and then a pre-authorized or auth-code offer; (2) nested flows, an OpenID4VP presentation inside the VCI authorization step and the only option that conforms to VCI 1.0; (3) the IAE or First-Party Apps, which is 1.1 or proprietary. It says the variants "may not be compatible" and differ in binding security and context switches | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/issues/730 | OpenID Foundation DCP WG (GitHub) | 2026-04-15 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | capability
- On 2026-05-21 mickrau reported that Variant 2 (nested) works with both the iOS and Android EUDI reference wallets. Wallet requirements: keep the VCI flow state while the nested VP runs, and open the authorization `request_uri` and the VP `redirect_uri` in the same user agent with session sharing. The preferred user agent is an Android Custom Tab (or ASWebAuthenticationSession / SFSafariViewController on iOS); the default browser is a "hard context switch". Claimed advantage: the HTTP session securely binds the issuance to the presentation | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/issues/730 | OpenID Foundation DCP WG (GitHub) | 2026-05-21 | accessed 2026-11-06 | med (single implementer's report) | capability
- Pitfall: Animo/Paradym (TimoGlastra, 2026-05-21) does not support Variant 2 when it means "opening the browser, which then redirects to the wallet again, as that would lose the OID4VCI session". Paradym instead uses the older SPRIND/BMI First-Party-Apps-based PDI flow. Implementations are not cross-compatible | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/issues/730 | OpenID Foundation DCP WG (GitHub) | 2026-05-21 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | capability
- Security pitfall, relevant to a derived-credential design: in presentation during issuance the subject claims come from the presented credential while the holder key comes from the issuance proof, and nothing cryptographically links the two. Issue #742 shows a Tamarin-verified cross-session attack in which a credential carries the victim's claims but is bound to the attacker's key. Proposed fixes are a holder-key-continuity check or a presenter-signed commitment. The issue targets the 1.1 IAE, but the same claims/key split exists in the 1.0 nested redirect variant [inference, not stated in the issue] | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/issues/742 | OpenID Foundation DCP WG (GitHub) | 2026-05-30 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high (issue content); low (applicability to Variant 2 is my inference) | spec
- Spec-level pitfall: issue #473 (2025-05-01 WG call) notes that the flow only addresses the case where the same wallet holds the credential to present and receives the new one. The case where the PID is in a different wallet is explicitly left unsolved | https://github.com/openid/OpenID4VCI/issues/473 | OpenID Foundation DCP WG (GitHub) | 2025-05-01 | accessed 2026-11-06 | high | spec
